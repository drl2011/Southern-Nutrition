Southern Nutrition — Saved Delivery Address Update

What changed:
- Logged-in customers now have one saved/default delivery address stored in Cloudflare D1.
- Saved fields: street, apartment/unit, city, state, ZIP, business/workplace, delivery instructions.
- When a logged-in customer returns on another device and signs in, the saved address auto-fills.
- The address is automatically saved when the logged-in customer clicks Continue to secure checkout.
- Guests still work normally; their address is not stored in D1.
- The database columns are created automatically by the Worker the first time this feature is used.

No manual SQL migration is required.
