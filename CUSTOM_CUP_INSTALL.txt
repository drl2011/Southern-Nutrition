Southern Nutrition — Custom Cup Patch

This patch does NOT redesign the site.

It adds:
- A clear Southern Nutrition cup full of ice inside Build Your Own.
- The exact custom flavor list from your menu sheet:
  Cherry, Mango, Strawberry, Pina Colada, Margarita, Melon,
  Blackberry, Orange, Grape, Pineapple, Lemonaid, Watermelon.
- First flavor fills the cup with that flavor color.
- Additional selected flavors blend the cup into a new color.
- Removing a flavor recalculates the color immediately.
- The cup uses the real Southern Nutrition logo cropped from the existing Hot Mess asset.

Files to add to the existing website:
1. custom-cup.js
2. assets/custom-cup-logo.png

Then add this ONE line immediately after the existing:
<script src="app.js"></script>

<script src="custom-cup.js"></script>

Nothing else on the site needs to change.
