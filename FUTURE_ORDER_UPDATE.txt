Southern Nutrition — Future Order Scheduling

Customers may now place and pay for orders at any time of day.

Rules:
- Requested delivery time is required.
- Delivery time must be in the future.
- Delivery time must be between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM Central.
- Example: an order placed at 9:00 PM tonight for 10:00 AM tomorrow is accepted.
- The browser validates the requested time before checkout.
- The Worker validates it again before Square payment, so the rule cannot be bypassed from the browser.

This update keeps the saved-address feature intact.
