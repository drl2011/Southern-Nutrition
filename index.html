Good to know

Frequently asked

When do you deliver?

Launch schedule is set for weekends. Exact hours and delivery zones can be added before launch.

Can I place a group order?

Yes. Add multiple drinks to one cart and use one workplace or delivery address. Orders of 10 or more drinks automatically get $1 off every drink; add-ons remain regular price.

How does the free drink work?

Every paid standard drink earns one reward credit. After nine paid drinks, the next standard drink is free.

How much caffeine is in a loaded tea?

Caffeine varies by recipe and add-ons. Loaded teas contain caffeine. Selecting Extra Liftoff adds additional caffeine; check the product labeling and ask us if you need exact amounts for a specific drink.
