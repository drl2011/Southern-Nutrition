Southern Nutrition — Square Orders + Sales Tax

Checkout now:
1. Calculates tax through Square before the customer pays.
2. Shows the Square-calculated sales tax and final total in checkout.
3. Creates a real Square Order with the drink line items.
4. Creates a scheduled DELIVERY fulfillment for the selected 30-minute window.
5. Sends the customer's delivery address/contact details to the Square Order.
6. Associates the card payment with that Square Order.
7. Records the tip separately as Square tip money.
8. Keeps the 10+ drink discount.
9. Keeps the D1 delivery-slot reservation.

Tax is NOT hard-coded. Square's configured Catalog Tax is used. The applicable
Square Catalog Tax must be enabled for custom/ad-hoc amounts. This avoids
guessing or hard-coding an incorrect local tax rate.
