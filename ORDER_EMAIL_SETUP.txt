Southern Nutrition — New Order Email Alerts

Recipient: Ashley.alarconlewis@gmail.com
Sender: orders@getsouthernnutrition.com

After a successful Square payment, the Worker sends a best-effort new-order email containing:
- delivery date/window
- customer name, phone, and email
- full delivery address and workplace
- delivery instructions and order notes
- every drink/item
- subtotal, discount, sales tax, tip, and total
- Square order and payment IDs

The payment is never reversed or reported as failed solely because an email cannot be delivered. Email failures are logged in Cloudflare.

Cloudflare setup required before email can send:
1. Compute & AI > Email Service > Email Sending.
2. Onboard getsouthernnutrition.com as a sending domain.
3. Ensure Ashley.alarconlewis@gmail.com is an allowed/verified destination if Cloudflare prompts for verification.
4. Deploy this package so the EMAIL send binding in wrangler.jsonc is applied.
