SOUTHERN NUTRITION WEBSITE — STARTER VERSION

Files:
- index.html
- styles.css
- app.js

What already works:
- Responsive website for desktop/mobile
- Loaded tea and iced coffee menu
- $12-$15 pricing examples
- Cart with quantity controls
- Pickup vs delivery selection
- Delivery address field
- Requested order time and notes
- Order-summary generator + copy button
- Buy 9 / get 10th free rewards concept
- Rewards profile saved locally in the customer's browser
- Menu category filters

Important:
This is a FRONT-END starter website. It does not actually charge cards, send orders to you, or sync loyalty across devices yet.

For a live business site, connect checkout and customer/rewards data to Square, Stripe, Toast, or another ordering platform. Square is likely the simplest fit for a small pickup/delivery drink business because payment, online ordering, POS, and loyalty can live together.
